Take a Peek at the World of Taekwondo


Taekwondo is a Korean martial art and combat sport. It is famous for its fast, high, and spinning kicks, and it is practiced globally as both a self-defense discipline and an official Olympic sport

Core Principles

    Focuses on mental discipline, and peace alongside physical training. Practitioners follow five core tenets.



  • Courtesy
  • Integrity
  • Perseverance
  • Self Control
  • Indomitable Spirit

Gameplay

Taekwondo consists of many game modes that emphasize their physical strength and strategic skills
